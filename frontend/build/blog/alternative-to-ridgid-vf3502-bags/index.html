Here's the one-time alternative: what it is, what it fits, and why you stop buying bags after this one.

The one-time alternative

Muk Buddy is a reusable bag that fits across all of those brands. It uses the same 2.5-inch inlet, works with RIDGID, DeWalt, Craftsman, Vacmaster, Shop-Vac, and Stanley wet/dry vacs, and costs $99 once.

At $12.49 per VF3502, Muk Buddy pays for itself after about eight bags. A crew burning two to three a week hits that in roughly a month. After that, the savings continue for as long as you own the vac.

Why it works better (not just cheaper)

The VF3502 is a solid disposable. But it's still a single-path bag. Dust and air share the same route. As it fills, fine drywall or concrete dust packs against the filter media and chokes airflow. Suction drops. The motor strains against a clog. By lunch, the bag is "full" even if it's barely loaded.

Muk Buddy is a 2-chamber design. Debris drops into the first chamber. Airflow passes through the second. The filter stays clean, suction stays steady, and the motor isn't fighting a packed filter. That's the difference between a vac that pulls strong all day and one that dies by noon.
