## What Muk Buddy is

Muk Buddy is one reusable filter assembly that replaces the disposable bag in a wet/dry shop vac. A disposable bag sends dust and air down the same path, so fine dust packs against the media and suction drops. Muk Buddy splits that job into two chambers. Heavy debris and fines drop out of the airflow path into the debris chamber, and clean air rises through filter-grade reusable fabric to the motor. That is why suction stays strong and the vac's own filter stays clean load after load.

When it is full, you dump it, rinse it if it is wet or muddy, and reload it. There is no bag to buy, no filter swap between wet and dry jobs, and no recurring cost. The full walkthrough is in [how a wet/dry vacuum works](/blog/how-wet-dry-vacuum-works/) and [multi-stage shop vacuum filtration](/blog/multi-stage-shop-vacuum-filtration/).

## Fit: which shop vacs Muk Buddy fits

Muk Buddy is made for 16-20 gallon wet/dry vacs with the standard 2.5 inch inlet. That covers most contractor-grade vacs from Shop-Vac, RIDGID, Craftsman, Stanley and DeWalt.

- **RIDGID:** fits the vacs that take VF3502 Size A bags, including the WD1270, WD14500, WD1680, WD1850, WD18510, WD1950 and WD1956. See [alternative to RIDGID VF3502 bags](/blog/alternative-to-ridgid-vf3502-bags/).
- **DeWalt:** fits the 16 gallon DXV16P, DXV16PA and DXV16S. It does not fit the smaller DXV12P or DXV14P. See [reusable bag for a DeWalt shop vac](/blog/reusable-bag-for-dewalt-shop-vac/).
- **Craftsman:** most 16-20 gallon Craftsman wet/dry vacs use the same 2.5 inch inlet. See [reusable shop vac bag for DeWalt and Craftsman](/blog/reusable-shop-vac-bag-for-dewalt-craftsman/).
- **Does not fit:** 5 gallon vacs, or any vac under 16 gallons.

It is both jobs in one reusable assembly. Muk Buddy takes the place of the disposable bag and uses a 2-chamber design so debris drops out before air reaches the vac's filter. The filter stays clean and the motor stays protected.

Yes. Muk Buddy is a wet/dry accessory. On wet jobs liquid collects in the bottom chamber and the fabric breathes, so the vac's filter does not see the moisture. Rinse it, drain it and reload it for the next job.
